SENSING SOLUTIONS • MULTI-AXIS FORCE/TORQUE

Giving Robots Tactile Sensitivity: 6-Axis Force/Torque Sensors

Eliminate part jamming, compensate for part tolerances, and maintain constant contact force during automated finishing.

Robots programmed purely on Cartesian coordinates fail when parts have dimensional variances or when delicate components must be pressed into place. INDUS 6-Axis Force/Torque Sensors (FTS Series) measure forces (Fx, Fy, Fz) and torques (Tx, Ty, Tz) simultaneously at the robot tool flange.

Built with monolithic monolithic alloy sensing rings and custom semiconductor strain gauges, our sensors provide high signal-to-noise ratios, sub-0.1 N resolution, and extreme mechanical stiffness that avoids compliant joint droop.

Paired with INDUS real-time impedance control algorithms over EtherCAT (1,000 Hz update rate), the robot can follow complex 3D contours with consistent buffing pressure or insert tight-tolerance splines without galling.

Key Engineering Advantages:
✓ Simultaneous 6-axis measurement (Fx, Fy, Fz, Tx, Ty, Tz) with sub-0.1 N resolution
✓ High mechanical overload protection (up to 500% of full measurement scale)
✓ Direct EtherCAT and Ethernet/IP output at 1 kHz update rate for low-latency servo loops
✓ Monolithic aircraft-grade aluminum alloy body with IP65 environmental sealing
