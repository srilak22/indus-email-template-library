Smart Grip Control: Servo-Electric Parallel Grippers

Replace rigid pneumatic grippers with intelligent, programmable electric clamping for high-mix assembly lines.

Traditional pneumatic grippers lack feedback: they clamp with maximum, uncontrolled force, risking part cracking or dropping fragile assemblies. The INDUS EG-Series Smart Servo-Electric Parallel Grippers provide micro-controlled force regulation and continuous position monitoring.

Featuring integrated brushless motors, precision ground leadscrews, and absolute magnetic encoders, users can program finger clamping force between 10 N and 500 N in 1 N increments, alongside millimeter-accurate stroke presets.

Equipped with native IO-Link and EtherCAT bus options, the gripper transmits real-time grip verification, part diameter measurements, and component drop warnings directly to the master controller.

Key Engineering Advantages:
✓ Programmable gripping force (10 N to 500 N) with ±2 N steady-state precision
✓ Continuous finger position feedback for real-time dimensional part inspection
✓ Integrated drop detection and grip confirmation without external proximity sensors
✓ Compact ISO 9409-1 robot flange mount with internal cable pass-through
