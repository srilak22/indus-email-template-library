Case Analysis
AI ROBOTICS • RANDOM BIN-PICKING

Zero-Orientation Parts Handling: Edge AI Robotic Bin-Picking

Eliminate expensive mechanical vibratory bowl feeders and automate raw casting feeding with deep-learning 3D vision.

Feeding raw forgings, tangled springs, and stamped brackets from bulk bins has long been a notorious automation bottleneck. Mechanical bowl feeders jam frequently and cannot accommodate part redesigns.

INDUS Edge AI Visual Guidance combines high-accuracy 3D point-cloud imaging with trained neural network segmentation. In less than 350 milliseconds, the system identifies target parts, calculates precise 6-DOF pick poses, and validates collision-free robot tool paths.

Operating on our rugged Edge AI industrial compute units with hardware tensor acceleration, the solution requires zero cloud connectivity, ensuring deterministic 1.2-second total cycle times on shop floors.

Key Engineering Advantages:
✓ Pre-trained deep neural networks for overlapping, reflective, and dark cast-iron parts
✓ Automated collision avoidance planning checking robot wrist, gripper, and bin walls
✓ Fast 350 ms inference and path planning time for sustained line productivity
✓ No mechanical tooling changeovers required when switching part variants
