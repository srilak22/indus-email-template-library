Scale Factory Automation Without Upfront CapEx: INDUS Leasing

Overcome capital expenditure freezes and deploy high-performance robotics with flexible, tax-advantaged monthly operating leases.

In volatile economic environments, tight capital expenditure budgets often delay critical automation investments, leaving plants vulnerable to labor shortages and rising operating costs.

The INDUS Industrial Robotics Rental & Leasing Program allows manufacturers to deploy cutting-edge robotic automation immediately while preserving cash reserves. Choose from flexible 12-month to 60-month operating leases with fixed, predictable monthly payments.

Every leased system includes comprehensive bumper-to-bumper warranty coverage, scheduled preventative maintenance visits, and options to upgrade to newer robotic models at the end of the term or purchase the equipment at fair market value.

Key Engineering Advantages:
✓ 100% financing covering hardware, end-of-arm tooling, and integration engineering
✓ Flexible lease durations from 12 to 60 months with custom seasonal payment structures
✓ OpEx tax deductibility: lease payments typically qualify as 100% deductible operating expenses
✓ End-of-term flexibility: upgrade to next-gen robotics, extend lease, or purchase equipment
