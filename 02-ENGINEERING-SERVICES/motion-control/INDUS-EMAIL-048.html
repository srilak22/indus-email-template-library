Case Study: 0.05 µm In-Position Stability for 300mm Wafer Inspection

How advanced servo loop tuning and active anti-resonance filters eliminated acoustic jitter and doubled inspection throughput.

A leading semiconductor metrology OEM in California faced severe velocity jitter and extended settling times on a 300 mm wafer inspection tool. Ambient cleanroom air-handling vibration and cable drag caused the direct-drive optical stage to hunt within a ±0.45 µm window.

INDUS motion control engineers were engaged to retune the multi-axis stage. We implemented an adaptive notch filter bank to suppress high-frequency mechanical resonance and developed an inverse-model acceleration feedforward controller.

The results exceeded specifications: in-position stability tightened to an astonishing ±0.05 µm (50 nanometers), while step-and-settle duration dropped from 85 ms to 22 ms. The customer doubled wafer inspection throughput from 35 to 70 wafers per hour.

Key Engineering Advantages:
✓ In-position settling stability improved by 89% (from ±0.45 µm down to ±0.05 µm)
✓ Step-and-settle time reduced from 85 ms to 22 ms across 10 mm indexing steps
✓ Inspection tool throughput doubled from 35 to 70 wafers per hour
✓ Zero hardware mechanical redesign required—optimization delivered entirely via control tuning
