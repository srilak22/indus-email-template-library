Trajectory Optimization & Jerk Suppression Services

Unlock hidden speed and sub-micron path repeatability in existing robotic and CNC platforms through expert motion tuning.

Many automated workcells suffer from excessive vibration, acoustic squeal, or sluggish cycle times simply because the default trapezoidal acceleration profiles excite mechanical resonances in the robot frame and tooling.

INDUS Motion Control Optimization Services bring deterministic trajectory science to your plant floor. Our motion specialists employ dynamic frequency-response analyzers (FRAs) to map structural transfer functions and identify anti-resonance pole-zero pairs.

We implement high-order continuous jerk-bounded S-curves, adaptive friction feedforward compensation, and multi-axis look-ahead algorithms that maximize tool-center-point (TCP) velocity through sharp corners without contour overshoot.

Key Engineering Advantages:
✓ Comprehensive structural frequency response and modal vibration analysis
✓ Implementation of 5th-order jerk-bounded (S-curve) kinematic trajectory profilers
✓ Adaptive friction and gravity feedforward compensation eliminating steady-state lag
✓ Proven 15% to 30% reduction in motion cycle times while reducing gear train wear
