Case Study: 32% Faster CNC Machine Tending in Automotive Powertrain

How INDUS application engineering eliminated spindle idle time through dual-gripper kinematics and blended trajectory paths.

A Tier-1 automotive powertrain supplier in Michigan was struggling with spindle idle times during the machining of cast aluminum transmission housings. Single-gripper loading created a 24-second door-open spindle wait cycle, severely capping overall line output.

INDUS application engineers performed a full dynamic audit. We redesigned the end-effector into a balanced dual-action servo gripper and re-modeled the 6-axis robot trajectory using continuous S-curve velocity blending, allowing raw part loading and finished part extraction in a single continuous sweep.

The door-open cycle time plummeted from 24.2 seconds to 16.4 seconds—a verified 32.2% reduction. Spindle utilization surged from 71% to 89%, yielding an additional 148 finished housings per shift across the 4-machine CNC cell.

Key Engineering Advantages:
✓ 32.2% reduction in CNC spindle idle time (from 24.2 s down to 16.4 s door-open duration)
✓ Spindle utilization increased from 71% to 89% without adding machine tools
✓ Custom dual-gripper mass matrix re-balanced to preserve Axis 4 and 5 reducer longevity
✓ Full capital payback achieved in just 4.2 months based on increased component shipment volume
