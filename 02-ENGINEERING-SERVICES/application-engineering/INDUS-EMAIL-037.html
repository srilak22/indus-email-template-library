Deterministic Feasibility: INDUS Application Engineering Services

Validate your robotic automation concepts before capital expenditure with rigorous physics-based simulation and sizing.

Selecting robotic manipulators and drive packages based solely on catalogue headline numbers frequently leads to costly undersizing, thermal overloads, and failed cycle-time guarantees.

INDUS Application Engineering provides end-to-end feasibility modeling. Using dynamic multi-body physics solvers and accurate motor-gearbox thermal models, our senior application architects analyze your exact motion profiles, end-effector mass matrices, and cable drag resistance.

Clients receive a comprehensive Engineering Validation Dossier containing 3D clearance envelopes, joint torque utilization margins, expected component lifetimes, and cycle-time benchmarks before a single purchase order is cut.

Key Engineering Advantages:
✓ Complete 3D kinematic reach, singularity analysis, and collision envelope verification
✓ Dynamic multi-body torque, inertia, and motor thermal rise calculations
✓ Deterministic cycle-time guarantees with simulated ±2% field correlation
✓ Impartial component trade-off analysis comparing Cartesian, SCARA, and articulated arms
