Your Software, Our Precision Hardware: Open-Architecture Integration

Never get locked into rigid vendor software stacks: control INDUS high-torque joints using your own C++, Python, or PLC algorithms.

Machine builders frequently encounter vendor lock-in: proprietary robot controllers force you into closed programming languages that prevent deep integration with your custom machine HMI and proprietary process software.

INDUS champions open-architecture industrial automation. Our servo drives and kinematic joint modules communicate over standard EtherCAT (CiA 402 profile), PROFINET IRT, and ROS 2 with microsecond deterministic update cycles.

Your software team maintains complete control over high-level trajectory generation, specialized kinematic transforms, and operator user interfaces, while our drive hardware handles deterministic current, velocity, and position closing.

✓ Eliminates expensive vendor software licenses and proprietary teach pendants
